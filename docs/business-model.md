# Business Model: Subsistence Crop Farming Household Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-6310`
- ISCO-08: `6310`
- Occupation: Subsistence Crop Farmers
- Social impact: food-security, household-livelihood, crop-resilience

## Customer

- subsistence-farming households
- household farming cooperatives / village extension programs

## Offer

- household labor/task scheduling coordination
- planting/harvest/yield-record logging
- seeds/tools supply-order coordination
- livelihood-concern surfacing to the farmer and household for human review

## Revenue

- low-cost per-household coordination fee (subsidized/cooperative pricing
  where applicable, reflecting subsistence-household ability to pay)
- extension-program or cooperative retainer

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a planting/harvest-timing agronomic decision,
  ever
- no override of the farmer's own judgment about their household's crops,
  ever
- flagged livelihood concerns (crop-failure risk, food-security risk,
  drought risk, pest-infestation risk) always route to human sign-off,
  regardless of confidence — this is the distinct livelihood-vulnerability
  dimension of subsistence farming: a crop failure here threatens the
  household's own food supply, not just a commercial outcome
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
